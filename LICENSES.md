## Data

All evaluation data is synthetic, generated by `eval/gen_cases.py` from a fixed
seed. No real, scraped, licensed or third-party dataset is included or was used.

- Personal names are drawn from a hardcoded list in the generator and do not
  refer to real individuals.
- Email addresses use `example.com`, reserved for documentation by RFC 2606.
- No customer, company or production data of any kind appears in this
  submission.
